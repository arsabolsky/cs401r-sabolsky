Pre-Lab 4 — SageMaker Training Quota Setup (Retired)

Status: Retired Wed Sep 30. No action required, and nothing to submit.

This pre-lab asked you to request an on-demand SageMaker training quota increase before Lab 4. You no longer need one. Lab 4's pipeline now trains on managed spot capacity, which fits within the default quota every new AWS account gets.

Why it changed

Quota AWS default on a new account
ml.m5.large for training job usage (on-demand, L-611FA074) 0
ml.m5.large for spot training job usage (spot, L-29688C85) 4

On-demand training quota is zero for every instance type, which is why this pre-lab existed. Spot training quota is not. Lab 4's training estimator now sets use_spot_instances=True and max_wait=3600, so the job runs on a brand-new account without a quota request.

We verified this on AWS on Sep 30. The full Lab 4 pipeline ran on spot ml.m5.large: it trained, registered the model, took 140 seconds, and was billed for 54.

If you already did it

Nothing is wasted, and there's nothing to undo.

  • A pending or approved on-demand request does no harm. A quota costs nothing unless you run jobs against it. You can leave it as it is.
  • A file you already submitted stays on record. It isn't graded, and it won't count against you.

What still matters in Lab 4

Two things carry over from this pre-lab:

  1. Keep use_spot_instances=True in the estimator. Remove it and the job falls back to on-demand, and you're back to a quota of zero.
  2. Check the right quota. get-service-quota reports what is applied to your account; get-aws-default-service-quota reports what a new account starts with. They can differ, and the difference is how instructors ship labs that work on their account and fail on yours.
aws service-quotas get-service-quota --service-code sagemaker \
  --quota-code L-29688C85 --query 'Quota.Value' --output text

Any value of 1 or more is enough for Lab 4.
